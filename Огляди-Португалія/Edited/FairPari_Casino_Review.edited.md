## Bonus Overview
The draft lists a welcome package up to €1,500 and 150 free spins, a €10 minimum deposit and 35x wagering. It does not fully specify the wagering basis or deposit-stage allocation. We retain the figures with that limitation rather than filling the gaps with assumptions.

Contribution is the decisive detail. A €10 qualifying bet at 100% contribution counts €10 towards a requirement; at 20%, it counts €2. These are illustrative rates, not confirmed Fair GAMES rates. Faster contribution would affect progress through the requirement, not the probability of winning a bet.

The supplied review also flags excluded games and cryptocurrency deposits being ineligible for the welcome bonus. Confirm the payment method, activation steps and current exclusions before depositing. A sports offer should not be used to fill missing casino terms.

## Limitations
This is not a simple offer to judge from the headline alone. Game eligibility, contribution, payment method and activation can each determine whether play counts towards the bonus. Missing one of those rules can make an apparently attractive multiplier misleading.

Public player feedback is especially limited. Our existing Trustpilot snapshot has three reviews, and Casino Guru does not supply a user score in the consulted snapshot. That is insufficient to substantiate broad claims about consistently good support, mobile quality or withdrawals.
