## Bonus Overview
The retained draft describes three deposits up to €3,000 in total, 100 free spins and a €10 minimum deposit. It lists 35x, 30x and 40x wagering depending on the offer, but does not establish a complete mapping between each multiplier, stage and qualifying balance.

That missing mapping matters. For illustration, a 35x requirement on €100 of bonus funds means €3,500 in eligible bets; the same multiplier on a €100 deposit plus €100 bonus means €7,000. We do not assume which basis applies to Leon without the specific terms.

The draft also flags manual activation and bonus cash-out caps. Before choosing a stage, check when activation must happen, which games contribute, the deadline and the cap applicable to that offer. Do not assume that accepting the first stage makes the next two automatic.
