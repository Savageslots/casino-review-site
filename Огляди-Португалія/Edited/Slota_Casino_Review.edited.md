## Bonus Overview
The draft terms are 575% up to €5,750 plus 500 free spins, a €20 minimum deposit, 30x wagering on bonus funds and 45x on free-spin winnings. The combined percentage does not reveal the match offered at each deposit stage; that allocation still needs to be checked in the promotion rules.

The two wagering figures describe different balances. For illustration, €100 of bonus funds at 30x would require €3,000 in eligible bets. €20 won from free spins at 45x would require €900. Neither amount is a prediction of losses, a promised payout or confirmation that both balances can be used together.

Maximum stake, expiry, eligible games and any conversion cap can change the usefulness of the package. In particular, a game that contributes only partly to wagering will take more betting activity to clear the same requirement.

## Limitations
The most useful caution comes from the split in public feedback. A positive account on one platform does not cancel a withdrawal complaint on another. The samples are small, and neither gives us a reliable average withdrawal time.

The draft’s claims about a Curaçao licence, mobile quality and VIP value are not treated here as independently verified findings. Before using a promotion, check the operator’s legal details and withdrawal rules, including the documents required and limits on bonus winnings.
